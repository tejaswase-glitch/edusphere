# Student manual
1. **Student Registration** on the homepage: name, mobile, email, course, year, password. Status = *pending*.
2. Wait for Admin approval. Until then you cannot open lectures, materials or classes. If rejected or suspended, contact the administrator.
3. **Student Login**. You stay signed in on your device until you press Logout.
4. **Dashboard**: lecture count, completed, progress %, Continue Learning, courses, next live class, recent activity.
5. **Recorded Lectures**: tap a lecture to watch inside EduSphere; press *Mark as completed* when finished.
6. **Study Materials**: filter by subject; Open / download (links last 90 seconds).
7. **Live Classes**: *Join Google Meet*. Cancelled classes show "Cancelled" without a Join button; changed times show "Rescheduled". Times display in your local time zone.
8. **My Profile**: update details, change password. Please do not share lecture or class links.
