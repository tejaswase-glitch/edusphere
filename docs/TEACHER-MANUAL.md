# Teacher manual
Accounts are created only by the Admin. Open the email, set your password, then **Teacher Login**.
- **Dashboard**: your counts, upcoming classes, shortcuts.
- **My Courses**: create/edit/(de)activate your own courses.
- **Study Materials**: upload PDF, DOC/DOCX, PPT/PPTX, XLS/XLSX or images, max 50 MB each. Publish/unpublish. You can delete only your own files (confirmation asked, deletion is logged).
- **Recorded Lectures**: paste a YouTube *Unlisted* link or ID, choose subject, optional thumbnail, tick Published. Edit/unpublish/delete only your own. Unlisted links can be shared by anyone who has them; this is not DRM.
- **Live Classes**: title, subject, Google Meet link (`https://meet.google.com/...`), date/time in your local time zone. **Edit / reschedule** keeps one record and students see "Rescheduled". **Cancel class** keeps the history and removes the Join button. Use Google Meet host admission controls; EduSphere cannot stop an approved student forwarding a link.
- **Record Screen**: tick consent, Start, choose a screen/window/tab (tick *share tab audio* if wanted), Stop, preview, Download (.webm usually). Then upload to YouTube as Unlisted and add it under Recorded Lectures. Student cameras are never requested. Other Meet participants' voices are not guaranteed to be recorded. On Android use the phone's screen recorder instead. Google Meet's own recording needs an eligible paid Workspace plan.
- **My Profile / Logout** in the sidebar.
