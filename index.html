About EduSphere

EduSphere brings students and teachers together in one place. Teachers publish recorded lectures, schedule live classes on Google Meet or Jitsi and share study materials. Students register, get approved by the Admin, and then learn at their own pace from any device.

Teacher accounts are created only by the Admin. Learning content stays behind your login.
